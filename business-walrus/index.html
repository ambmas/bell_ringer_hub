How to play · Teacher instructions
  1. Make groups of entrepreneurs. Reveal the Business Walrus's product challenge for the round.
  2. Each group designs a product that meets the request. Draw a surprise from the hat and incorporate it into the pitch.
  3. Give groups 3 minutes to prepare, then 60 seconds per group to pitch their idea.
  4. After everyone pitches, use anonymous votes or your own judgment to award the round.
  5. The first team to earn $3 billion in investments wins.
